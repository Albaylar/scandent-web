Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · Applies to ScanDent iOS, Apple Watch, and scandent-web

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanDent (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and website (the “Service”). We designed ScanDent with privacy in mind: your photos stay on your device by default, are sent only when you request an analysis, and are never saved on our servers.

1. Information we collect

Photos and camera data

When you run a dental scan, food scan, or Fast Check, you choose photos to analyze. These images typically show your mouth and teeth and may include parts of your face (for example lips or cheeks visible in a smile selfie). Images are compressed on your device before upload. After you give in-app permission, we send them to our secure proxy server solely to perform the analysis you requested. We do not use your photos for advertising profiles.

Face data

What we collect: Smile / mouth photographs you capture or select for a Dental Score, whitening simulation, or related analysis. We do not collect Face ID data, TrueDepth meshes, biometric face templates, or a persistent face-geometry model.

On-device (not shared): To help you frame your mouth correctly and to render optional on-device whitening previews, the camera may use Apple's Vision framework to detect facial landmarks (such as lip position) in real time. That landmark processing happens entirely on your device, is used only momentarily, is never stored, and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Shared with AI (with your permission): The photos you deliberately submit for analysis are sent to our proxy and third-party AI providers as described in section 3. The app asks for your explicit consent before the first upload. You can decline; in that case no photo is sent.

Retention & deletion: Our server analyzes your photos in memory and never saves them: they are not written to disk, a database, or our logs, and are discarded as soon as your result is returned. Our AI providers (Google Gemini, OpenAI) may keep a copy for a limited time for safety checks only (OpenAI: up to 30 days); see section 3. Your scores and scan history are stored only on your iPhone until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Analysis results

Scores, metrics, recommendations, and related metadata returned by our AI systems may be stored locally on your device (for example, scan history and progress charts). We do not maintain a centralized user account database in the MVP version of the App.

Usage and device information

We may collect limited technical data such as:

  • App version, device type, and operating system;
  • Language preference and in-app settings;
  • Crash logs or diagnostics if you opt in through Apple’s systems;
  • Subscription status via Apple and our payment/analytics partner (Adapty);
  • Anonymous product analytics (see below).

Anonymous product analytics

To understand which features work and where the app fails, ScanDent sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck (servers in the EU), for example that a scan finished, a screen or the paywall was shown, or a badge was earned. Events carry only coarse values, such as a score range like “60–69” or a duration bucket. They never contain photos, names, email addresses, or free text, and they are tied to an anonymized identifier that cannot be traced back to you. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

Apple Watch sync

If you use the ScanDent Apple Watch companion, plan data, streaks, and checklist progress may sync between your iPhone and Watch via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework and a shared App Group container on your devices. This data stays on your devices and is not uploaded to our servers.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We receive subscription status and product identifiers through Adapty to unlock Pro features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • Provide AI analysis, scores, simulations, and in-app features you request;
  • Enforce free-tier usage limits and prevent abuse of our API;
  • Manage subscriptions and restore purchases;
  • Improve reliability, performance, and product experience;
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests.

3. AI processing and third parties

Before your photos are sent for analysis for the first time, the app shows an in-app disclosure that explains what is sent (your captured mouth/teeth photos), who receives it (our secure server and the third-party AI providers below), and asks for your explicit permission to continue. You can decline, in which case no photos are sent.

To deliver analysis, images and prompts may be processed by:

  • Our proxy infrastructure (hosted on Vercel) — receives photos, calls AI models, returns JSON results;
  • Google Gemini — primary vision/analysis provider;
  • OpenAI — fallback vision/analysis and image editing for whitening simulations;
  • Adapty — subscription management and paywall analytics;
  • TelemetryDeck — anonymous product analytics (EU servers), no photos or personal content;
  • Apple — App Store billing, TestFlight, and platform services.

Our proxy processes photos in memory only and does not store them after the request completes. The AI providers process data under their own API terms and privacy policies: OpenAI does not use API inputs to train its models and may retain them for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring; Google may log Gemini API requests for a limited period to detect misuse. We do not share your photos with anyone else and do not use them for advertising.

4. Data retention

  • On your device: Scan history, plans, streaks, and preferences remain until you delete the App or clear data.
  • On our servers: Photos are never saved. Our proxy keeps only short-lived request counters for rate limits and free-tier quotas, keyed by a random app install ID or your IP address. They contain no images or results and expire automatically (food-scan quota counters within about a week).
  • At our AI providers: Limited retention for safety checks under their API terms (OpenAI: up to 30 days), as described in section 3.
  • Support email: If you contact us, we retain correspondence as long as needed to resolve your request.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you request;
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, and product improvement;
  • Consent — camera/photo access and optional notifications (requested in the App).

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because most data stays on your device, deleting the App removes locally stored scan history. For subscription or support questions, contact us at the email below.

7. Children

ScanDent is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, app-level API keys, rate limiting, and industry-standard practices to protect data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use the Service at your own risk.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in the App where appropriate.
